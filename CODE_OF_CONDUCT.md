# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pagevo to be a project anyone can participate in — filing an issue, asking a
question, or sending a patch — without being harassed or belittled. Everyone contributing
to this project is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and be kind. Technical disagreement is welcome and useful; contempt is not.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume the person on the other end is competent, busy, and working from different
  context than you.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. "No" to a patch is not a judgement of the
  person who wrote it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual attention or imagery in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
code comments — and anywhere someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem via a private message to a maintainer (see the repository's contact
options, or open a private security advisory if no other private channel is available).
Reports are handled privately, and your identity won't be shared with the person reported
without your agreement.

Maintainers will do what's proportionate: a private word, editing or removing content, a
temporary cooling-off period, or a permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who don't
follow this document are held to it too.
